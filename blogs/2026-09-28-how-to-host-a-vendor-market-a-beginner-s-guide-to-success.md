Vendor markets are vibrant, community-driven events where local creators, crafters, and small businesses come together to showcase their goods. Whether you're planning a seasonal pop-up, a recurring art fair, or a one-day maker market, learning how to host a vendor market successfully takes more than picking a date and sending out invites. A vendor market is a curated gathering of independent sellers (think artists, bakers, designers, and hobbyists) who set up individual booths or tables to sell their goods.
