Back to school event ideas move from "someday" to "right now" faster than most organizers expect. By midsummer, many PTAs and school groups are already choosing dates, reserving spaces, recruiting volunteers, and deciding what fits their budget. This list includes 20 back to school event ideas for schools and PTAs, organized by event type.
