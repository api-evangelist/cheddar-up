The PTA holiday shop is one of those feel-good school traditions that brings smiles and a little seasonal sparkle. Kids pick out gifts for their families on their own, and your school raises money and builds community spirit. As the PTA member running it, you’ll have to make a lot of decisions, like who supplies the inventory, what to price at $1 versus $5, how many volunteers each shift needs, and how kids pay at the register.
