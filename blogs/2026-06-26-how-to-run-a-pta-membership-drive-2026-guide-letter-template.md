A strong PTA membership drive sets up everything else your PTA does. Membership dues support programs, events, and initiatives throughout the school year, while a growing membership base helps build engagement across your school community. This guide walks you through how to run a PTA membership drive from start to finish, including when to launch, how to increase sign ups, and a PTA membership drive letter template you can customize and send to families.
