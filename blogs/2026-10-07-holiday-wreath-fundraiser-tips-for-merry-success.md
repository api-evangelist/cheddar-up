A holiday wreath fundraiser is a festive, low-risk way for schools, PTAs, sports teams, and nonprofits to raise money during the winter season. With a little planning and the right online fundraising platform, you can turn a wreath sale into a joyful, community-powered fundraising success. This guide covers the full Christmas wreath sale, from picking products and a supplier to running pickup day, plus how to add Christmas trees to the same sale without setting up a tree lot.
