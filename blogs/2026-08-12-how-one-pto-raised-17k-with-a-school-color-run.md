Melissa Knox is part of the CECA PTO at a TK-8 charter school in California. While her role focuses on social media and communications, she ended up taking the lead on one of the school’s biggest fundraisers: a Color Run Against Cancer. The idea started with the school principal, who had previously helped organize a district-wide runathon for the American Cancer Society.
